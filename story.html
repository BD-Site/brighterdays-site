Our story

We opened in 2020 with a simple idea: great flavors, live music and good vibes. Years later, that's still the whole plan.

Music is the heartbeat here, and every beer we brew has a song in mind. Our stage welcomes everything from funk and soul to jam-band improv and laid-back country.

Three distinct spaces, one easy vibe: our air-conditioned taproom, the open-air garage with a view of the brewhouse, and a big deck right off the Pinellas Trail with Adirondack chairs and umbrellas. And our walk-up window means bikers and trail walkers are never far from a cold pint.

We're family-friendly and dog-friendly. Since 2022, every March we've hosted the 5K for Autism, which funds the Heart 4 Kids scholarship.
