Our story

We opened in 2020 with one idea: a big patio, cold beer, and live music most nights. Six years later, that's still the whole plan.

Music is the heartbeat here, and every beer we brew is named after a song. Thursdays and First Fridays bring bands to the taproom, from funk and soul to jam-band improv and laid-back country.

Three rooms, one easy vibe: the indoor bar, the open-air garage with the game on, and a big deck right off the Pinellas Trail, with Adirondack chairs and umbrellas. Our walk-up window means bikers and trail walkers are as much a part of the crowd as the regulars.

We're family-friendly and dog-friendly. Since 2022, every March we've hosted the 5K for Autism, which funds the Heart 4 Kids scholarship.
